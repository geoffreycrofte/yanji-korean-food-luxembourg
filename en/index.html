Small menu, big heart

At Yanji, we serve simple, generous Korean food in the heart of Luxembourg’s Gare district. A small menu, a caring team and warming dishes: kimbap, bibimbap, tteokbokki, slow-cooked soups and crispy fried chicken.

Eat in or take away, for a lunch break or a savoury snack: drop by Monday to Saturday.

                                How to eat bibimbap?비벼 먹어요

                                “Bibim” means mixing and “bap” means rice. The secret: mix everything before you dig in!

                                1. Add the chilli sauceA little or a lot of gochujang, to taste.
                                2. Mix everything togetherRice, vegetables, meat and egg: everything should be well coated.
                                3. Enjoy!Time to savour a moment of simple happiness.
